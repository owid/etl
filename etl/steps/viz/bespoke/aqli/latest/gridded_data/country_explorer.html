Includes natural sources. These estimates include mineral dust and sea salt as well as pollution from human activities. Country averages weight concentrations by where people live; grid cells show local concentrations. The WHO annual guideline is 5 µg/m³.

Country detail uses the same fine source resolution throughout 1998–2024. Zoom in to distinguish individual cells. Coverage extends from 60°S to 70°N; areas outside that range have no grid data. The initial view focuses on the largest landmass.

Data source: SatPM V6.GL.03, Atmospheric Composition Analysis Group. Country and state/province boundaries: GADM via AQLI. City labels: GHSL urban centres (2020), with larger centres prioritized where labels overlap. Prototype.
